An AI-powered design generation pipeline that produces on-brand assets at scale. Built to maintain visual consistency across marketing materials, social posts, and product interfaces.

**What it is.** An internal tool that turns design decisions into a system anyone on the team can operate. Brand rules — colour, type, layout, voice — are encoded once, and the tool generates assets that follow them: social posts, banners, announcement graphics. The output looks like it came from the design team, because the design team's judgement is baked into the generator.

**The problem it solves.** Ecosystem marketing produces a relentless volume of assets: campaign graphics, community updates, event announcements, localized variants. A design team can set the standard but can't personally produce every derivative. The usual failure mode is drift — each derivative gets a little less on-brand until the ecosystem looks like five different companies. This tool makes consistency the default instead of a review step.

**Who it's for.** Marketing and community teams who need assets now, and designers who want to spend their time on new work rather than resizing last week's graphic. Non-designers get guardrails; designers get leverage.

**How it's built.** A generation pipeline that combines templated layout logic with AI image generation for visual content, all constrained by the brand system. Parameters — format, campaign, message — go in; a finished, on-brand asset comes out. Where generation alone would drift, templates hold the line.

**A concrete use case.** A campaign launches in three regions with three headline variants. Instead of a day of manual production, the community lead generates the full set — same layout, same brand, correct copy — in minutes, and the designer reviews rather than rebuilds.

**What it demonstrates.** The useful version of AI in design isn't generating random images — it's encoding taste so it can be reproduced. That's the bet this tool was built to test, and it held.
