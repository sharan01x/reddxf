An ad-supported micro-app suite offering free online tools with a focus on proper design. From converters to generators, every tool is built with attention to UX and visual consistency.

**What it is.** reddtools is a growing collection of single-purpose web tools. Each one does exactly one job and gets out of the way: a converter converts, a generator generates. There are no accounts, no upsells, no cookie-consent mazes — you arrive at the tool, use it, and leave. The whole suite is free, supported by unobtrusive ads rather than subscriptions.

**Why "designed properly" matters.** Most free online tool sites are visually hostile: stacked ad banners, fake download buttons, tools rebuilt in 2009 and never touched since. reddtools starts from the opposite premise — that a tool you use for thirty seconds still deserves a clean interface, readable typography, and fast load times. The design discipline is the differentiator, not an afterthought.

**Who it's for.** Anyone who googles "convert x to y" or "generate a z" and just wants a working answer. Designers, developers, students, and office workers who use these micro-tools dozens of times a week and are tired of wading through junk sites to do it.

**How it's built.** Every tool is a lightweight static page with client-side logic where possible — instant results with nothing to install and nothing waiting on a server. New tools ship continuously, chosen by looking at what people actually search for and doing that one thing better than the existing results.

**A concrete use case.** You need a specific file format converted right now, before a meeting. On reddtools you land on the tool, drop your file in, get the result, and get back to work — in under a minute, without an ad banner covering the download button.

**Where it's heading.** More tools, steady growth, and the long bet that a well-designed free suite earns loyalty that junk sites can't buy. Visit [reddtools.com](https://reddtools.com) to see the current suite.
