CreaPay is a payments product for creators: tips that land in seconds from anywhere, a digital-goods checkout that stays on the creator's own site, and one analytics view of every payment coming in. Built on the Avail ecosystem and run by Redd Crew.

**What it is.** Two products on one rail. A tipping flow that rides on a creator's content — a QR in the corner of a video, a link in a bio — where a fan pays with no sign-up, no forms, and no friction. And a digital-goods store that lives on the creator's own website: one line of embed code, an unlimited product catalogue, and a branded checkout overlay so the fan never leaves the page. Every tip and sale lands in one dashboard — one ledger, split by source, ready for tax time.

**The problem it solves.** Existing creator payment tools take a heavy cut and add friction at exactly the moment money should move: the fan who has to enter a phone number and abandons the tip, the creator who sends fans off to a marketplace — a Patreon, a Gumroad — and breaks their own brand to make a sale. Every extra step is a payment that doesn't happen, and the income that creators lose to friction and drop-off is invisible to them.

**How it works.** Payment is global by design: a fan pays their way, the creator receives theirs — settled in seconds, worldwide. A creator's income stays private; supporters see their own tip, never the creator's ledger. Getting started is one line of code pasted into a page — no Stripe integration, no storefront to configure, no catalogue to keep in sync across platforms.

**A concrete use case.** A photographer publishes a video and wants a tip link on it. She pastes the tip QR into her video and one script tag into her site. From that moment fans tip from any country in seconds, her preset pack sells inside her own site with instant download delivery, and every payment — tip or sale — shows up in one dashboard.

**Who it's for.** Independent creators who publish across platforms but get paid through a patchwork of marketplaces and donation pages: YouTubers, streamers, photographers, writers, developers selling presets, ebooks and courses.

**Where it stands.** V1 scope: tipping plus one digital-good type, with a flat 8% all-in fee that scales down with volume. Built as an Avail-ecosystem venture, run by Redd Crew.
