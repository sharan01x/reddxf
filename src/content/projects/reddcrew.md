Redd Crew is a service that gives a business its own crew of AI agents, running in the company's own cloud. The crew handles the operational work a team shouldn't be doing — the chasing, drafting, updating, reporting — so the people already trusted do the work only they can do.

**What it is.** A standing crew of specialised agents with distinct roles — an executor, a strategist, an observer, and more — that work in weekly and daily cycles. Each one is a specialist: one runs the queue, one analyses what works and tunes the approach, one watches the signals. They run in your cloud, on your accounts, with your data staying yours.

**The problem it solves.** New hires aren't the only way to grow, but growing teams keep reaching for them for work that isn't worth a person: following up, drafting routine comms, keeping records current, assembling reports. Redd Crew takes that layer off the team without adding headcount — roughly a fifth of one fully-loaded employee in running costs, covering a slice of everyone's workload instead of one job description.

**How it works.** A setup sprint scopes the crew to the business, wires it into the tools the team already uses, and defines the loops the agents run. From then on the crew works autonomously in the background — you talk to it in chat, it manages the tasks silently. No dashboards to babysit.

**A concrete use case.** A design team's operational layer — client follow-ups, status updates, timesheet nudges, weekly reports — runs itself. The people do the design.

**Who it's for.** Small and mid-sized teams whose operational overhead grows faster than the team, and who want automation they own rather than another SaaS tenant.
